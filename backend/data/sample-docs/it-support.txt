IT Support Guidelines

If you encounter issues with your workstation, please try restarting it first.
To request a hardware upgrade or replacement (e.g., new monitor, keyboard), submit a request to the IT department.
All company laptops are managed via MDM. Do not attempt to uninstall the management profile.
VPN access is required for accessing internal staging servers. You can download the VPN client from the IT self-service portal.
For urgent issues (e.g., account lockouts, data breaches), call the 24/7 IT emergency hotline at ext 5555.
