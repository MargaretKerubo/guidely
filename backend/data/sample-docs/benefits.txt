Benefits Overview

Guidely offers a comprehensive benefits package to all full-time employees.
Health Insurance: We cover 100% of employee premiums for our base health plan. Dependents can be added at a subsidized rate.
Retirement: The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 5% of your salary.
Wellness: Employees receive a $50 monthly stipend for gym memberships or wellness apps.
Commuter: Pre-tax commuter benefits are available for public transit and parking.
