Company Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year.
Sick leave is provided at 10 days per year.
To request time off, please submit a request through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for planned vacations.
For unexpected illnesses, notify your manager before 9:00 AM on the day of your absence.
Maternity leave is 16 weeks fully paid, and paternity leave is 6 weeks fully paid.
