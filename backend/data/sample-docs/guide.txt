System Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Guidely! To get started:
1. Log into your company email account.
2. Go to the Okta dashboard and set up two-factor authentication (2FA).
3. Access the internal intranet and review the Employee Handbook.
4. Request access to specific tools (Jira, GitHub, AWS) through the IT ticketing system.
If you experience any issues, open a ticket at support.guidely.local.
