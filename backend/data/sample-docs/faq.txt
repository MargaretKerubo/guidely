Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: You can reset your password by visiting the IT Helpdesk portal and clicking on "Forgot Password". A link will be sent to your alternative email.

Q: What are the core working hours?
A: Core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Employees are expected to be available for meetings during this time.

Q: Can I work from home?
A: Yes, our company supports a hybrid work model. You can work from home up to 3 days a week, pending approval from your direct manager.
